MSE

Mean Squared Error measures average pixel-level difference from the original.

PSNR

Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio expresses reconstruction error in decibels.

SSIM

Structural Similarity estimates perceptual structure similarity. May be unavailable if its dependency cannot load.

Metrics appear after the corresponding image is processed. Values are never hardcoded.

?

What happens behind the scenes?

Salt-and-pepper noise randomly turns some pixels black or white. A standard median filter uses one fixed neighborhood size. The adaptive median filter checks local minimum, maximum, and median values, then grows its window until it finds a suitable median or reaches the configured maximum.
